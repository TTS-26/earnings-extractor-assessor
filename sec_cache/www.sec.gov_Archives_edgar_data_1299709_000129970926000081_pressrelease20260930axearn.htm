Axos Financial, Inc. Reports First Quarter Fiscal Year 2027 Results
LAS VEGAS, NV – (BUSINESS WIRE) – October 29, 2026 – Axos Financial, Inc. (NYSE and NYSE Texas: AX) (“Axos” or the “Company”) today announced unaudited financial results for the first fiscal quarter ended September 30, 2026. Net income was $129.6 million and diluted earnings per share (“EPS”) was $2.25 for the quarter ended September 30, 2026. Net income for the quarter ended September 30, 2025 was $112.4 million and diluted EPS was $1.94. Adjusted earnings and adjusted earnings per diluted common share (“Adjusted EPS”), non-GAAP measures described further below, increased $14.5 million to $134.8 million and increased $0.27 to $2.34, respectively, for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, compared to $120.4 million and $2.07, respectively, for the quarter ended September 30, 2025.

“Net interest margin increased by 10 basis points quarter-over-quarter, resulting in a 16.8% annualized growth in net interest income,” stated Greg Garrabrants, President and Chief Executive Officer of Axos. “Excluding net paydowns in single-family mortgage and warehouse, multifamily and small balance commercial real estate, ending loans grew by approximately $563 million this quarter. Our loan pipeline remains solid, and we expect a few large loans that did not close in the September 2026 quarter to drive an acceleration in overall loan growth in the December 2026 quarter.”

“Double-digit growth in net interest income and non-interest income, coupled with good operating expense management, resulted in a 16.0% year-over-year increase in diluted earnings per share in our fiscal first quarter of 2027,” said Derrick Walsh, Chief Financial Officer of Axos. “We charged off two non-performing C&I loans that we set aside specific loan loss reserves for in prior quarters. Excluding those two C&I charge-offs, our annualized net charge-offs to average loans was 0.08% in the three months ended September 30, 2026. We remain well-reserved given our historically low levels of net charge-offs.”

First Quarter Fiscal 2027 Income Statement Summary
Net income was $129.6 million and diluted EPS was $2.25 for the three months ended September 30, 2026, compared to net income of $112.4 million and diluted EPS of $1.94 for the three months ended September 30, 2025. Net interest income increased $40.2 million, or 13.8%, for the three months ended September 30, 2026, compared to the three months ended September 30, 2025, primarily due to an increase in interest income earned on loans, partially offset by a decrease in interest income on deposits at other financial institutions, and increases in interest expense on advances from the Federal Home Loan Bank and secured financings.
The provision for credit losses was $24.5 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, compared to $17.3 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025. The provision for credit losses for the three months ended September 30, 2026, was primarily driven by certain individually assessed loans, partially offset by a benefit in the Commercial Real Estate portfolio and lower expected losses on unfunded lending commitments, both resulting from an improved macroeconomic outlook reflected in the forecast scenarios used in the allowance for credit losses model.
Non-interest income increased to $63.8 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, compared to $32.3 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025. The increase was primarily due to operating lease rental and other income from the acquisition of Verdant Commercial Capital, LLC (“Verdant”) on September 30, 2025, commercial office complex operating lease rental income and higher prepayment penalty income.
Non-interest expense, comprised of various operating expenses, increased $39.2 million to $195.4 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, from $156.2 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025. The increase was primarily due to higher depreciation and amortization expense, mainly attributable to Verdant and the acquisition of deposits, an increase in salaries and related costs, general and administrative expense and higher data and operational processing expense, partially offset by lower professional and contracted services expense.
Balance Sheet Summary
Axos’ total assets increased by $0.9 billion, or 2.9%, to $30.8 billion, at September 30, 2026, from $30.0 billion at June 30, 2026, primarily attributable to an increase in cash and cash equivalents and loans, partially offset by a decrease in available-for-sale-securities. Total liabilities increased by $0.8 billion, or 2.8%, to $27.6 billion at September 30, 2026, from $26.8 billion at June 30, 2026, primarily attributable to higher deposit balances, including the acquisition of deposits from Capital One, partially offset by lower advances from the Federal Home Loan Bank and secured financings. Stockholders’ equity increased $122.4 million, or 3.9%, to $3.3 billion at September 30, 2026, from $3.2 billion at June 30, 2026, primarily due to net income of $129.6 million.

About Axos Financial, Inc. and Subsidiaries
Axos Financial, Inc., with approximately $30.8 billion in consolidated assets as of September 30, 2026, is the holding company for Axos Bank, Axos Clearing LLC and Axos Invest, Inc. Axos Bank provides consumer and business banking products nationwide through its low-cost distribution channels and affinity partners. Axos Clearing LLC (including its business division Axos Advisor Services), with approximately $48.0 billion of assets under custody and/or administration as of September 30, 2026, and Axos Invest, Inc., provide comprehensive securities clearing services to introducing broker-dealers and registered investment advisor correspondents, and digital investment advisory services to retail investors, respectively. Axos Financial, Inc.’s common stock is listed on the NYSE and NYSE Texas under the symbol “AX” and is a component of the Russell 2000® Index and the S&P SmallCap 600® Index, among other indices. For more information on Axos Financial, Inc., please visit http://investors.axosfinancial.com.

Use of Non-GAAP Financial Measures
In addition to the results presented in accordance with accounting principles generally accepted in the United States of America (“GAAP”), this release includes non-GAAP financial measures such as adjusted earnings, adjusted earnings per diluted common share, and tangible book value per common share. Non-GAAP financial measures have inherent limitations, may not be comparable to similarly titled measures used by other companies and are not audited. Readers should be aware of these limitations and should be cautious as to their reliance on such measures. Although we believe the non-GAAP financial measures disclosed in this release enhance investors’ understanding of our business and performance, these non-GAAP measures should not be considered in isolation, or as a substitute for GAAP basis financial measures.
We define “adjusted earnings”, a non-GAAP financial measure, as net income without the after-tax impact of non-recurring acquisition-related items (including amortization of intangible assets related to acquisitions) and other costs (unusual or non-recurring charges). Adjusted EPS, a non-GAAP financial measure, is calculated by dividing non-GAAP adjusted earnings by the average number of diluted common shares outstanding during the period. We believe the non-GAAP measures of adjusted earnings and adjusted EPS provide useful information about Axos’ operating performance. We believe excluding the non-recurring acquisition-related costs and other costs provides investors with an alternative understanding of Axos’ core business.
