BorgWarner Reports Strong Third Quarter 2026 Results
Increases 2026 Adjusted EPS Guidance
Announces 9 Awards Across Portfolio to Support Long-Term Profitable Growth

Auburn Hills, Michigan, October 29, 2026 – BorgWarner Inc. (NYSE: BWA) today reported third quarter results for 2026.

Third Quarter Results and Business Highlights
•BorgWarner (the “Company”) achieved a U.S. GAAP operating margin of 8.7% during the third quarter of 2026, or an increase of 180 basis points, compared with the third quarter of 2025. The Company achieved an adjusted operating margin of 10.9%, or an increase of 20 basis points, compared with the third quarter of 2025. The Company’s continued focus on cost controls allowed it to deliver strong performance despite a lower industry production environment.
•The Company successfully executed a debt tender and make-whole redemption to reduce its gross debt balance by $864 million during the third quarter of 2026.
•The Company repurchased approximately $175 million of its outstanding shares and paid a $35 million cash dividend to its shareholders during the third quarter of 2026.
•The Company continued to make progress in its product readiness across its portfolio offerings for the data center and industrial markets. Additionally, the Company has accelerated its quoting activity for its industrial solutions.

New Business Awards Across Portfolio
The Company secured multiple new business awards that are expected to support its long-term profitable growth, including the following:
Foundational Awards
•Engine control module award with a global OEM for a new internal combustion engine pickup truck platform. Production is expected to begin in 2029.
•Three conquest turbocharger awards with a global powertrain leader for light vehicle gasoline platforms in South America and Europe. Production is expected to begin in 2027.
eProduct Awards
•First aluminum-wire eMotor program award with a major Chinese OEM for hybrid vehicles, including generator and traction motors for auxiliary drive applications. Production is expected to begin in 2026.
•Three eMotor awards with leading OEMs in China for range-extended, battery-electric and hybrid vehicle applications. Production is expected to begin between 2027 and 2028.
•High-voltage inverter award with a global OEM for its next-generation battery-electric vehicle programs. This award builds on an established customer relationship.

Third Quarter Summary:
•U.S. GAAP net sales of $3,487 million, a decrease of approximately 2.9% compared with the third quarter of 2025.

Net sales were $3,487 million for the third quarter of 2026, a decrease of approximately 2.9% compared with the third quarter of 2025. This decrease was primarily driven by declining market production volumes and lower Battery Energy Systems segment sales. Net earnings for the third quarter of 2026 were $221 million, compared with net earnings of $158 million for the third quarter of 2025. Net earnings per diluted share for the third quarter of 2026 were $1.08, up 47.9% from $0.73 per diluted share for the third quarter of 2025. Adjusted net earnings per diluted share for the third quarter of 2026 were $1.39, up 12.1% from adjusted net earnings per diluted share of $1.24 for the third quarter of 2025. Adjusted net earnings for the third quarter of 2026 excluded net non-comparable items of $(0.31) per diluted share, while adjusted net earnings for the third quarter of 2025 excluded net non-comparable items of $(0.51) per diluted share. These and other non-comparable items are listed in the table above, which is provided by the Company for comparison with other results and the most directly comparable U.S. GAAP measures. The increase in adjusted net earnings per diluted share was primarily due to a lower full year effective tax rate and the impact of a lower share count resulting from share repurchases in 2025 and 2026.

Full Year 2026 Guidance Update: The Company increased its 2026 adjusted earnings per share guidance due to the expected impacts of the Company's share repurchases and a decline in its full year effective tax rate.
At the midpoint of its 2026 guidance, the Company expects to deliver another year of adjusted operating margin expansion and adjusted earnings per share growth despite the Company’s expectation that its weighted light vehicle market will be down 2% to down 1% and a $10 million to $15 million industrial R&D investment to support long-term profitable growth. Net sales are expected to be in the range of $14.0 billion to $14.2 billion in 2026, compared with 2025 net sales of approximately $14.3 billion. The Company’s net sales guidance range has been narrowed from its previous forecast of $14.0 billion to $14.3 billion due to lower industry production expectations at the high-end of the Company’s previous guidance. The Company’s net sales guidance implies a year-over-year change in organic net sales of down 4% to down 2.5%. The Company’s net sales guidance includes an expected year-over-year sales decline of approximately $250 million in the Company’s Battery Energy Systems segment, which represents approximately a 1.7% headwind to organic net sales growth in 2026. Foreign currencies are expected to result in a year-over-year increase in sales of approximately $250 million primarily due to the strengthening of the Euro and Chinese Renminbi against the U.S. dollar.

U.S. GAAP operating margin is expected to be in the range of 9.3% to 9.6% in 2026. Excluding the impact of non-comparable items and the add-back of intangible asset amortization expense, adjusted operating margin is expected to be in the range of 10.7% to 10.9%. U.S. GAAP net earnings are expected to be within the range of $4.65 to $4.84 per diluted share. Excluding the impact of non-comparable items, adjusted net earnings are expected to be in the range of $5.20 to $5.35 per diluted share, compared with the Company’s previous adjusted net earnings guidance range of $5.05 to $5.30 per diluted share. This increase is due to the expected impacts of the Company's share repurchases and a decline in its full year effective tax rate. Full year operating cash flow is expected to be in the range of $1,600 million to $1,700 million, while free cash flow is expected to be in the range of $900 million to $1,100 million, consistent with the Company’s previous guidance.

Adjusted Operating Income and Adjusted Operating Margin
The Company defines adjusted operating income as operating income adjusted to exclude the impact of restructuring expense, merger, acquisition and divestiture expense, intangible asset amortization expense, other net expenses, discontinued operations and other gains and losses not reflective of the Company’s ongoing operations. Adjusted operating margin is defined as adjusted operating income divided by net sales.

Adjusted Net Earnings
The Company defines adjusted net earnings as net earnings attributable to the Company, adjusted to eliminate the impact of restructuring expense, merger, acquisition and divestiture expense, other net expenses, discontinued operations and other gains and losses not reflective of the Company’s ongoing operations and related tax effects. The impact of intangible asset amortization expense continues to be included in adjusted net earnings.

Adjusted Earnings per Diluted Share
The Company defines adjusted earnings per diluted share as earnings per diluted share adjusted to eliminate the impact of restructuring expense, merger, acquisition and divestiture expense, other net expenses, discontinued operations and other gains and losses not reflective of the Company’s ongoing operations and related tax effects. The impact of intangible asset amortization expense continues to be included in adjusted earnings per share.

Free Cash Flow
The Company defines free cash flow as net cash provided by operating activities minus capital expenditures, net of customer advances related to capital expenditures. The Company believes this measure is useful to both management and investors in evaluating the Company’s ability to service and repay its debt.
