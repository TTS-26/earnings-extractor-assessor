Eledon Pharmaceuticals Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results and Recent Business Highlights

Planned global Phase 3 LEGACY trial of tegoprubart in kidney transplantation remains on track to initiate in Q4 2026

FDA cleared the Company’s IND application for a planned registrational path study of tegoprubart in islet cell transplantation for patients with type 1 diabetes

FDA Fast Track designation granted to tegoprubart for the prevention of rejection in kidney transplantation

Cash, cash equivalents and short-term investments of $69.7 million with cash runway into Q2 2027

 

IRVINE, Calif., November 12, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Eledon Pharmaceuticals, Inc. (“Eledon”) (Nasdaq: ELDN) today reported its third quarter 2026 operating and financial results and provided recent business highlights.

“We continue to execute across our key strategic priorities and make meaningful progress in the tegoprubart clinical program across multiple transplant types, including plans to initiate our global Phase 3 LEGACY trial in kidney transplantation by year end,” said David-Alexandre C. Gros, M.D., Chief Executive Officer of Eledon. “New patient-reported outcomes data from the Phase 2 BESTOW program further strengthened the evidence supporting tegoprubart’s differentiated profile compared to today’s standard of care immunosuppression therapy, while investigator-initiated studies and compassionate-use protocols help expand our understanding of tegoprubart and its therapeutic potential in different transplant settings where significant unmet needs remain.”

Third Quarter 2026 Business Highlights

Kidney Transplantation

•
New patient-reported outcomes data from the Phase 2 BESTOW clinical program were presented at the American Society of Nephrology (ASN) Kidney Week 2026 Annual Meeting in October 2026. Results demonstrated sustained improvements in quality of life and symptom burden in kidney transplant recipients treated with tegoprubart compared with tacrolimus, today’s standard of care immunosuppression therapy. Tegoprubart-treated patients demonstrated a mean improvement on the total Modified Transplant Symptom Occurrence and Symptom Distress Scale (MTSOSD-59R) at 12 months while tacrolimus-treated patients experienced a mean worsening of symptom occurrence. Patients treated with tegoprubart also experienced a statistically

significant improvement on the Kidney Disease Quality of Life Short Form (KDQOL-SF) Symptoms and Problems Scale through 12 months following transplantation compared with tacrolimus.
•
Updated long-term data from the Phase 2 BESTOW extension study were presented at the International Congress of the Transplantation Society (TTS) in September 2026. Tegoprubart-treated patients maintained statistically significantly higher mean estimated glomerular filtration rate (eGFR) at 18, 21, and 24 months, with an approximately 13 mL/min/1.73m2 advantage over tacrolimus at month 24 (71 vs. 58 mL/min/1.73m2 (95% CI: 2.6, 23.3)). There were no reported cases of biopsy-proven acute rejection (BPAR) in the tegoprubart arm beyond six months post-transplant compared with seven BPAR events in the tacrolimus arm.
•
The first highly sensitized kidney transplant patient was dosed with tegoprubart under a compassionate-use protocol at Duke University Medical Center. Highly sensitized patients requiring a kidney transplant face a substantial unmet need because pre-existing antibodies can significantly limit access to compatible donor organs and increase the risk of antibody-mediated rejection and graft loss post-transplant.
•
The third patient was treated as part of the investigator-initiated study evaluating tegoprubart for kidney transplant tolerance induction at Massachusetts General Hospital (MGH).
•
Tegoprubart received Fast Track designation from the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) for the prevention of rejection in kidney transplantation.

Islet Cell Transplantation

•
The FDA cleared the Investigational New Drug (IND) application for a planned Company-sponsored, registrational path clinical trial evaluating tegoprubart for the prevention of allograft rejection in type 1 diabetes (T1D) patients undergoing pancreatic islet cell transplantation.
•
The first patients were enrolled in an investigator-initiated clinical trial underway at the University of Chicago Medicine Transplant Institute, evaluating tegoprubart for the prevention of allograft rejection in patients with T1D and renal dysfunction from chronic kidney disease who are receiving an islet cell transplant.
•
The first islet cell transplant recipient was dosed with tegoprubart under a compassionate-use protocol allowing patients to switch from tacrolimus-based immunosuppression therapy due to calcineurin inhibitor-related renal dysfunction.
•
The University of Chicago Medicine Transplant Institute expanded its ongoing investigator-initiated study evaluating tegoprubart as the core immunosuppressant for T1D patients undergoing islet cell transplantation by enrolling three additional subjects. This will help build on previously reported study results in the 12 patients treated to date, who have achieved 100% insulin independence and demonstrated stable islet graft function through 22 months of follow-up with a favorable tolerability profile. No evidence of nephrotoxicity, hypertension, or

Anticipated Upcoming Milestones

The Company anticipates the following milestones over the next 12 months:

•
Initiate global Phase 3 LEGACY trial of tegoprubart in kidney transplantation in the fourth quarter of 2026. The LEGACY trial is expected to enroll approximately 600 patients, with a primary endpoint of non-inferiority of tegoprubart versus tacrolimus at 52 weeks based on a composite of BPAR, graft loss, and death.
•
Initiate a Company-sponsored, registrational path study evaluating tegoprubart in T1D patients undergoing islet cell transplantation.
•
Initiate an investigator-led study evaluating tegoprubart for the prevention of organ rejection in patients receiving a de novo liver transplant.
•
Receive FDA regulatory guidance on the path to market for tegoprubart in kidney xenotransplantation.

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results

Cash, cash equivalents and short-term investments totaled $69.7 million as of September 30, 2026, compared to $133.3 million as of December 31, 2025. The Company expects cash, cash equivalents and short-term investments currently on hand to fund operations into Q2 2027.

Research and development (R&D) expenses for the third quarter of 2026 were $17.8 million, including $0.9 million of non-cash stock-based compensation expense, compared to $15.0 million, including $1.1 million of non-cash stock-based compensation expense, for the comparable period in 2025.

General and administrative (G&A) expenses for the third quarter of 2026 were $3.9 million, including $1.0 million of non-cash stock-based compensation expense, compared to $4.1 million, including $1.4 million of non-cash stock-based compensation expense, for the comparable period in 2025.

Net loss for the third quarter of 2026 was $3.9 million, or $0.03 per basic common share, compared to a net loss of $17.5 million, or $0.21 per basic common share, for the comparable period in 2025. Net loss in the third quarter of 2026 included a non-cash gain of $17.0 million from changes in the fair value of warrant liabilities, while the 2025 net loss included a non-cash gain of $0.6 million from such changes. Excluding the non-cash items related to changes in the fair value of warrant liabilities, Eledon would have recorded a net loss of $21.0 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, and $18.0 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025.

About Eledon Pharmaceuticals and tegoprubart

Eledon Pharmaceuticals, Inc. is a clinical stage biotechnology company that is developing immune-modulating therapies for the management and treatment of life-threatening conditions. The Company’s lead investigational product is tegoprubart, an anti-CD40L antibody with high affinity for the CD40 Ligand, a well-validated biological target that has broad therapeutic potential. The central role of CD40L signaling in both adaptive and innate immune cell activation and function positions it as an attractive target for non-lymphocyte depleting, immunomodulatory therapeutic intervention. The Company is building upon a deep historical knowledge of anti-CD40L biology to conduct preclinical and clinical studies in kidney allograft transplantation, xenotransplantation, islet cell transplantation, liver transplantation, and amyotrophic lateral sclerosis (ALS). Eledon is headquartered in Irvine, California. For more information, please visit the Company’s website at www.eledon.com.
