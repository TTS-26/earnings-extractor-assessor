RAVE Restaurant Group, Inc. Reports First Quarter 2027 Results
 
Dallas, Texas – RAVE Restaurant Group, Inc. (NASDAQ: RAVE) today reported financial results for the first quarter of fiscal 2027 ended September 27, 2026.
 
First Quarter Highlights:
 
•
The Company recorded net income of $0.7 million for the first quarter of fiscal 2027, a 3.1% increase from the same period of the prior year.
 
•
Income before taxes increased by 3.4% to $0.9 million for the first quarter of fiscal 2027 compared to the same period of the prior year.
 
•
Total revenue increased by $0.1 million to $3.3 million for the first quarter of fiscal 2027 compared to the same period of the prior year, a 3.4% increase.
 
•
Adjusted EBITDA was $0.8 million for the first quarter of fiscal 2027, unchanged from the same period of the prior year.
 
•
On a fully diluted basis, net income per share was $0.05 for the first quarter of fiscal 2027, unchanged from the same period of the prior year.
 
•
Pizza Inn domestic comparable store retail sales decreased 4.2% in the first quarter of fiscal 2027 compared to the same period of the prior year. First quarter prior year comparable sales increased 8.1%.
 
•
Pie Five domestic comparable store retail sales decreased 18.2% in the first quarter of fiscal 2027 compared to the same period of the prior year. First quarter prior year comparable sales decreased 9.1%.
 
•
Cash and short-term investments totaled $14.8 million on September 27, 2026.
 
•
Pizza Inn domestic unit count finished the quarter at 86, including 81 buffet locations.
 
•
Pizza Inn international unit count finished the quarter at 18.
 
•
Pie Five domestic unit count finished the quarter at 13.

“The first quarter did not meet our expectations as we faced a challenging macroeconomic pressures and lapping high comparable store sales of +8.1% at Pizza Inn in the first fiscal quarter of the prior year when we rolled out a value promotion centered around an eight dollar buffet at some Pizza Inn restaurants” said Brandon Solano, Chief Executive Officer of RAVE Restaurant Group, Inc.
 
Solano added, “And while we are not satisfied with the comparable store sales results at either Pie Five or Pizza Inn in the first quarter, we maintain confidence in our long-term strategy and have initiatives in place designed to sequentially improve Pizza Inn comparable store and total sales moving forward. We are excited about the product innovation coming in December and have earmarked advertising dollars to support the new product with a media campaign. Additionally, Pizza Inn continues to add net buffet store count with the successful opening in Gilbert, South Carolina during the first quarter and has opened a buffet location early in the second quarter in Warner Robins, Georgia. However, due to recent events with the multi-brand operator, we no longer anticipate any of the five new restaurants of the ten-unit development deal reported in the September 24, 2026 earnings release to open this fiscal year.”
 
Chief Financial Officer Jay Rooney added, “We continued to generate cash and further strengthened our balance sheet in the first quarter of fiscal year 2027. Cash generated from operating activities exceeded $1.1 million, helping build our combined cash and short-term asset balance to over $14.8 million at the end of the quarter. With no debt, a $2.9 million deferred tax asset, and a high level of liquidity we have built solid current financial strength and have given ourselves flexibility for the future.”
 
Non-GAAP Financial Measures
 
The Company’s financial statements are prepared in accordance with United States generally accepted accounting principles (“GAAP”). However, the Company also presents and discusses certain non-GAAP financial measures that it believes are useful to investors as measures of operating performance. Management may also use such non-GAAP financial measures in evaluating the effectiveness of business strategies and for planning and budgeting purposes. However, these non-GAAP financial measures should not be viewed as an alternative or substitute for their financial statements prepared in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles.
 
The Company considers EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA to be important supplemental measures of operating performance that are commonly used by securities analysts, investors and other parties interested in our industry. The Company believes that EBITDA is helpful to investors in evaluating its results of operations without the impact of expenses affected by financing methods, accounting methods and the tax environment. The Company believes that Adjusted EBITDA provides additional useful information to investors by excluding non-operational or non-recurring expenses to provide a measure of operating performance that is more comparable from period to period. Management also uses these non-GAAP financial measures for evaluating operating performance, assessing the effectiveness of business strategies, projecting future capital needs, budgeting and other planning purposes.
 
“EBITDA” represents earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortization. “Adjusted EBITDA” represents earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortization, stock compensation expense, severance, gain/loss on sale of assets, costs related to impairment and other lease charges, franchise default and closed store revenue/expense, and closed and non-operating store costs. A reconciliation of these non-GAAP financial measures to net income is included with the accompanying consolidated financial statements.

About RAVE Restaurant Group, Inc.
Dallas-based RAVE Restaurant Group [NASDAQ: RAVE] has inspired restaurant innovation and countless customer smiles with its trailblazing pizza concepts. The Company franchises, licenses and supplies Pie Five and Pizza Inn restaurants operating domestically and internationally. The Pizza Inn experience is unlike your typical buffet. Since 1958, Pizza Inn's house-made dough, house-shredded 100% whole milk mozzarella cheese, fresh ingredients and house-made signature sauce combined with friendly service solidified the brand to become America's favorite hometown pizza place. These, in addition to its small-town vibe, are the hallmarks of Pizza Inn restaurants. In 2011, RAVE introduced Pie Five Pizza, pioneering a fast-casual pizza brand that transformed the classic pizzeria into a concept offering personalization, sophisticated ingredients and speed. Pie Five's craft pizzas are baked fresh daily and feature house-made ingredients, creative recipes and craveable crust creations. For more information, visit www.raverg.com, and follow on Instagram @pizzainn and @piefivepizza.
