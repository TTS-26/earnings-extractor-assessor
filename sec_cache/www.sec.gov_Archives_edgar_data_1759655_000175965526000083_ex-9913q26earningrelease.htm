Expands VBC Footprint with Acquisition of American Choice Healthcare MSSP Business
This week, the Company announced the acquisition of American Choice Healthcare MSSP, LLC, a wholly-owned subsidiary of Cano Health. This Accountable Care Organization (ACO) will care for more than 36,000 attributed lives in the Medicare Shared Savings Program (MSSP), increasing VBC attributed lives in ten existing Privia states and adding lives in three new states, starting January 1, 2027.
Privia Health paid approximately $36 million at closing using its balance sheet cash. The transaction is expected to have minimal impact on all key metrics in 2026 and positively contribute to Attributed Lives and Adjusted EBITDA in 2027.
Exits Montana Partnership effective January 2027
Privia Health has agreed to end its partnership with Surgery Partners in Montana effective January 2027. The Company supports 69 providers and generated approximately $23 million in annual Practice Collections in Montana. This is expected to have no material impact on Adjusted EBITDA in 2027.
Capital Resources
Privia Health's balance sheet at September 30, 2026, included cash and cash equivalents of $446.3 million and no debt. The Company’s cash balance does not reflect the acquisition of American Choice Healthcare MSSP, LLC at the end of October 2026. We expect to end this year with at least $520 million in cash and cash equivalents, including the American Choice Healthcare transaction and assuming the 2025 MSSP cash settlement and related provider disbursements occur before year end based on current accruals.

About Privia Health
Privia Health™ is one of the largest physician enablement companies in the United States with a presence in 25 states and the District of Columbia. Privia builds scaled provider networks with primary-care centric medical
3

groups, risk-bearing entities, a physician-led governance structure, and the Privia Platform comprising an extensive suite of technology and service solutions. Privia collaborates with medical groups, health plans and health systems to optimize ~1,400 physician practices, improve the patient experience for 6.3+ million patients, and reward 5,800+ physicians and advanced practitioners for delivering high-value care.
Privia’s mission is to transform healthcare delivery to achieve better outcomes, lower costs, and improve the health of communities and the well-being of providers. For more information, visit priviahealth.com.
