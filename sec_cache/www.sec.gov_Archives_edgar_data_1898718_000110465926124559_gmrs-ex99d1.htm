GMR Solutions Inc. Reports Financial Results

for the Three and Nine Months Ended September 30, 2026

and Updated Full Year 2026 Guidance

LEWISVILLE, Texas (November 9, 2026) — GMR Solutions Inc. (“GMR” or the “Company”) (NYSE: GMRS), the nation’s largest provider of emergency medical services (“EMS”), today announced financial results for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026, and updated its full year 2026 financial guidance.

“The third quarter showcased the resiliency that is inherent in GMR’s scale, national footprint, and integrated model,” said Nick Loporcaro, Chairman and Chief Executive Officer. “Our ability to serve patients across a broad range of operations and geographies helped us navigate weather-related constraints and other external pressures while continuing to grow our core emergent services. Combined with the momentum we are seeing in Nurse Navigation and new market growth, these results reinforce our confidence in the strength of our platform and opportunities ahead.”

“We delivered strong financial performance in the third quarter, with revenue increasing 5.9% to $1,595.6 million, and net income increasing 81.7% to $135.6 million, despite weather-related pressure on flight activity, higher fuel and other inflationary costs, and the anticipated shift in payor mix,” said Brian Tierney, Chief Financial Officer. “Growth in our core emergent services, continued improvement in revenue per transport and disciplined cost management helped us absorb these headwinds while continuing to invest in the business and strengthen our financial position.”

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Highlights

●Net revenue of $1,595.6 million, up 5.9% compared to $1,506.9 million in the third quarter of 2025
●Net income of $135.6 million, up 81.7% compared to net income of $74.6 million in the third quarter of 2025
●Adjusted EBITDA(1) of $356.6 million, up 4.2% compared to $342.1 million in the third quarter of 2025
●Maintained a strong liquidity position of approximately $999.3 million, comprised of $298.0 million of cash and cash equivalents and approximately $701.3 million of available borrowing capacity on the ABL Facility as of September 30, 2026
●Reduced leverage and strengthened the Company’s balance sheet through a series of strategic capital transactions, including the repricing of its senior secured term loan due 2032 on September 17, 2026, which included the repayment of $200.0 million of senior secured term loan borrowings and a reduction to the applicable interest rate by 50 basis points.

About Global Medical Response

​

GMR is the nation’s largest provider of EMS, delivering EMS and other essential out-of-hospital care in rural and urban communities that represent more than 60% of the U.S. population. As the only national, fully integrated, air and ground EMS provider, GMR operates in approximately 1,400 counties across the country. A recognized innovator, GMR develops new solutions to meet evolving industry needs and expand access to high-quality care. With approximately 34,000 team members, GMR supports roughly 5.5 million patient encounters annually and performs a critical care intervention every 87 seconds. Its family of solutions includes ambulance EMS provider American Medical Response, as well as multiple air EMS organizations including Air Evac Lifeteam, REACH Air Medical Services, Guardian Flight, Med-Trans Corporation, and AirMed International.

Non-GAAP Financial Measures

This press release contains “non-GAAP financial measures,” which are financial measures that either exclude or include amounts that are not excluded or included in the most directly comparable measures calculated and presented in accordance with GAAP. Specifically, we make use of the non-GAAP financial measures “EBITDA,” “Adjusted EBITDA,” and “Adjusted Diluted EPS.”

We provide non-GAAP financial information to enhance the understanding of our GAAP financial information and it should be considered by the reader in addition to, but not instead of, the financial statements prepared in accordance with GAAP. Each non-GAAP financial measure is presented along with the corresponding GAAP measure so as not to imply that more emphasis should be placed on the non-GAAP measure. We believe that providing these non-GAAP measures in addition to the GAAP measures allows management, investors and other users of our financial information to more fully and accurately assess performance. The non-GAAP financial information presented may be determined or calculated differently by other companies and may not be directly comparable to that of other companies.

​

EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA

​

We define EBITDA as net income (loss) before interest expense, net, income tax provision (benefit), and depreciation and amortization. We define Adjusted EBITDA as EBITDA, as further adjusted to exclude management fees, stock-based compensation, professional fees and other expenses for non-recurring matters, debt refinancing fees paid to (received from) third parties and certain other items that we do not consider indicative of our ongoing operating performance.

​

Management uses EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA to supplement GAAP measures of performance in the evaluation of the effectiveness of our business strategies, to make budgeting decisions, to establish and award discretionary annual incentive compensation, to report compliance with certain covenants in our debt agreements and to compare our performance against that of peer companies using similar measures. Moreover, we present EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA because we believe that investors consider them to be important supplemental measures of our performance and believe these measures are frequently used by securities analysts, investors and other interested parties in the evaluation of companies in our industry.

​

​

Adjusted EBITDA is an analytical indicator used by management and the healthcare industry to evaluate company performance and allocate resources. Adjusted EBITDA should not be considered in isolation or as an alternative to net income (loss), cash flows from operations, investing or financing activities, or other financial statement data presented in the unaudited condensed consolidated financial statements as indicators of financial performance. Because Adjusted EBITDA is not a measure determined in accordance with GAAP and is thus susceptible to varying calculations, Adjusted EBITDA as presented may not be comparable to other similarly titled measures of other companies and may not be comparable to similarly titled measures used in debt compliance calculations. Net income (loss) is the financial measure calculated and presented in accordance with GAAP that is most comparable to Adjusted EBITDA, as defined.

​

Adjusted Diluted EPS

​

We define Adjusted Diluted EPS as net income (loss) available to common stockholders, adjusted for the items and related income tax effects shown in the reconciliation below, divided by adjusted diluted weighted-average common shares outstanding. Adjusted diluted shares reflect the inclusion of potentially dilutive stock awards and warrants excluded from GAAP diluted shares due to a net loss position, but do not include stock awards or warrants that are out of the money and whose inclusion would be antidilutive. We believe Adjusted Diluted EPS is useful to investors because it facilitates period-to-period comparisons of our earnings performance by excluding the effects of our pre-IPO preferred stock and other items that we do not consider indicative of our ongoing operating performance.
