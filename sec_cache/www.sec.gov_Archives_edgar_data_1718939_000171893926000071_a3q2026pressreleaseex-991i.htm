Exhibit 99.1
T STAMP INC
(‘Trust Stamp’ or ‘The Company’)
Nasdaq: IDAI
Trust Stamp provided a report for the nine months ending September 30, 2026 including a 37% increase in net revenue for Q3 and a 28% increase in net revenue for the nine-month period when compared to the comparable periods in 2025, with the growth in revenue significantly outpacing the growth in cash expenses
November 16, 2026, Atlanta, Georgia, United States: Trust Stamp (Nasdaq: IDAI) announced that:
1.Net recognised revenue for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 was $2.85 million, an increase of 28% from $2.23 million for the corresponding period in 2025 and 37% for Q3, primarily attributable to increased revenue from the Company's S&P 500 bank customer and $629 thousand recognized revenue during the nine months ended 30 September 2026 for work with a multinational telecommunications corporation, with an additional $294 thousand of work completed for that customer during the nine month period but not billed and recognized as at September 30, 2026. Additional contracted work and usage based revenue continues for that customer and will be billed and recognized in future quarters.
2.The Company also communicated a growing and maturing revenue pipeline from governmental and private sector customers. The Company routinely discounts the revenue pipeline for internal planning purposes and based on conservative estimates (including a 50% discount for projections relating to newer customers and minimum quantities for usage-based revenue) is planning based on the FY-2027 pipeline generating $8.17 million in booked revenue plus additional revenue from significant new engagements, and initiatives that are not yet capable of accurate projection as to revenue or timing.
3.The Company has previously provided information on two executed government-service sub-contracts in Nigeria and Ghana. Those contracts are the fruits of long term investment in developing business opportunities in the respective countries and each is a multi-year, multi-million dollar ARR opportunity with revenue projected to start in 2027. The company is currently negotiating additional government and private sector contract opportunities in both countries.
4.Total Operating Expenses were $9.44 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, included $2.47 million of non-cash expenses (an increase in non-cash expenses of $976 thousand compared to the corresponding period in 2025) an increase of $1.59 million compared to $7.85 million for the corresponding period in 2025 including a 9.7% increase in cash Operating Expenses. The increase in Operating Expenses included an estimated 5% in inflation related cost increases, up-front costs related to the engagements with the multinational telecommunications corporation, which are expected to continue yielding long-term-revenue together with one-time acquisition expenses related to Lexverify, internal and external development expenses related to the Wallet of Wallets (WoWTM), one-time expenses related to the financing completed in Q2, changes in fair value of notes payable, increases in depreciation and timing differences in recognition of RSU expenses, as well as increased cost of sales in tandem with the increase in both recognized and unbilled revenue.
5.Net Loss for the three months ended September 30, 2026 based on recognized revenue was $2.36 million including $1.00 million of non-cash expenses, compared to $1.91 million Net Loss including $631 thousand of non-cash expenses for the three months ended September 30, 2025 and reflects a 37% increase in recognized revenue versus a 5.1% increase in cash Operating Expenses.
6.Net Loss for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 based on recognized revenue was $7.29 million including $2.47 million of non-cash expenses, compared to $5.78 million Net Loss including $1.49 million of non-cash expenses for the nine months ended September 30, 2025 and reflects a 28% increase in recognized revenue versus a 9.7% increase in cash Operating Expenses.


7.For the purpose of clarifying cash utilization, the Company reiterated that the net loss included $2.47 million of non-cash expenses resulting in a net nine month cash burn of $4.82 million including a number of one-time expenses.
8.Basic and diluted net loss per share were $1.32 per share for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, compared to $2.28 per share for the corresponding period in 2025.
9.Cash and Cash Equivalents as of September 30, 2026 were $3.24 million which, with receivables and prepaid expenses, resulted in Total Current Assets of $6.17 million. Management believes that Current Assets together with ongoing revenue provide adequate resources for the company’s operations.
10.On November 10th 2026 the Company announced a new Knowing ConsentTM product for the authorization of Agentic-AI initiated transactions. On November 11th 2026 the Company announced Provisional Patent # 64/195,409 issued by the United States Patent and Trademark Office for the authorization process. Unlike theoretical frameworks for agent identity and authorization, the Company’s methodology is human-centric and ready for immediate use.
Gareth N. Genner, Chief Executive Officer of the Company commented: “The increase in recognized revenue while managing cash expenditure all without slowing investment in innovation and market development reflects a high level of performance on historic engagements as well as new clients from whom we expect to see a significant growth in billable revenue. Our engagements with the international telecommunications company and the exclusive sub-contracts for government services in Ghana and Nigeria each have the potential to eclipse the historic revenue from our largest engagement.
We will be announcing a significant development in our investment in the Sovereign-AI concept during Q4. About Trust Stamp
Trust Stamp is a global provider of AI-powered services for use in multiple sectors including banking and finance, regulatory compliance, government, healthcare, real estate, communications, and humanitarian services. Its technology empowers organizations via advanced solutions that reduce fraud, tokenize and secure data, securely authenticate users while protecting personal privacy, reduce friction in digital transactions, and increase operational efficiency, enabling customers to accelerate secure financial inclusion and reach and serve a broader base of users worldwide.
With team members from twenty-two nationalities in eight countries across North America, Europe, Asia, and Africa, Trust Stamp trades on the Nasdaq Capital Market (Nasdaq: IDAI).
