Non-GAAP Financial Measures We use certain financial measures which are not calculated and presented in accordance with U.S. generally accepted accounting principles (“GAAP”). These measures are focused on adjusting certain metrics used to measure our performance to exclude the impact of items or events that management believes are not reflective of core operating performance (collectively “Non-core adjustments”). Management uses such non-GAAP financial measures to help evaluate our business on a consistent basis, measure our performance, identify trends affecting our business, and assess our operational efficiencies. For the periods presented, Non-core adjustments include: (i) Fintech loan credit enhancement; (ii) Provision for credit losses on fintech loans; (iii) Restructuring costs; (iv) Legal settlement (reimbursement); and (v) Income tax benefits related to a one-time adjustment from purchased transferable tax credits that relate to prior tax years. Non-GAAP measures include: • Adjusted net income is calculated as: GAAP Net income, plus: (i) pre-tax Non-core adjustments, as defined above; (ii) Income tax expense or benefit related to Non-core adjustments; and (iii) Non-core adjustments to Income tax expense. • Core earnings per share is calculated as: Adjusted net income (Non-GAAP), divided by weighted-average diluted shares. • Core revenue is calculated as: the total of GAAP Net interest income and GAAP Non-interest income, less Fintech loan credit enhancement income. • Core non-interest income is calculated as: GAAP Non-interest-income less Fintech loan credit enhancement income. • Core non-interest expense is calculated as: GAAP Non-interest expense, less Non-core adjustments from Restructuring costs and Legal settlement (reimbursement). • Core income tax expense is calculated as: GAAP Income tax expense, less (i) Income tax expense or benefit related to Pre-tax Non-core adjustments; and (ii) Non-core adjustments to Income tax expense related to transferable tax credit carryback. • Core return on assets is calculated as: Adjusted net income (Non-GAAP), divided by Average assets, as reported. • Core return on equity is calculated as: Adjusted net income (Non-GAAP), divided by Average equity, as reported. • Core efficiency ratio is calculated as Core non-interest expense (Non-GAAP) divided by Core revenue (Non-GAAP). This ratio compares revenues generated with the amount of expense required to generate such revenues and may be used as one measure of overall efficiency. • Core non-interest income as a percentage of total revenue is calculated as: (i) Core Non-interest-income (Non-GAAP); divided by (ii) Core revenue (Non-GAAP). This ratio is used to compare the amount of non-interest income, which is primarily fee-based, to our total revenue each period to review the growth in our fee-based business. • Core fintech fees as a percentage of total revenue is calculated as: (i) GAAP Non-interest income – Total fintech fees; divided by (ii) Core revenue (Non-GAAP). This metric is used to review the growth in our fintech business, which is one of our key areas of focus. • Core FTS fee revenue is calculated as: (i) Non-interest income attributed to the results of our Fintech Solutions (FTS) segment; less (ii) Fintech loan credit enhancement income. This amount is used in the Operating Leverage calculation, as discussed below. • Core FTS expense is calculated as: (i) Non-interest expense amounts directly attributed to the operations of our FTS segment, as presented in ‘Direct non-interest expense’ in our Segment results disclosed in our Form 10-Q; plus (ii) Non-interest expense allocated to our FTS segment; less Legal settlement (reimbursement). This amount is used in the Operating Leverage calculation, as discussed below. • Operating Leverage is calculated as: (i) Change in Core FTS fee revenue, adjusted from the prior year; less (ii) Change in FTS expense from the prior year. This ratio measures the relationship between growth in the operating income of the FTS segment to the fixed and variable expenses of the segment. • Core FTS Expense / GDV (per thousand) is calculated as: (i) Core FTS expense; divided by (ii) total Prepaid, debit and credit card gross dollar volume (GDV). This ratio measure the GDV volume to the FTS segment expense base. We believe that these non-GAAP measures are useful performance metrics for management, investors, and lenders, because they provide a means to evaluate period-to-period comparisons of the Company’s financial performance without the effects of certain adjustments in accordance with GAAP, and unusual, one-time, non-ordinary course items that are not reflective of core operating performance. Non-GAAP financial measures should not be considered as an alternative to GAAP financial measures. They may not be indicative of the historical operating results of the Company nor are they intended to be predictive of potential future results. Investors should not consider non-GAAP financial measures in isolation or as a substitute for performance measures calculated in accordance with GAAP. Where non-GAAP financial measures are used, the most comparable GAAP financial measure, as well as the reconciliation of the non-GAAP financial measure to the most comparable GAAP financial measure, can be found in text or in the tables or attached to this presentation.
