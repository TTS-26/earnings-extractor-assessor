Cboe Global Markets Reports Results for Third Quarter 2026
Third Quarter Highlights*
•Record Diluted EPS for the Quarter of $4.08, Up 43 percent
•Adjusted Diluted EPS1 for the Quarter of $3.63, Up 36 percent
•Net Revenue for the Quarter of $712.2 million, Up 18 percent
•Increases 2026 Organic Total Net Revenue Growth Target2 to 'high teens' from 'mid to high teens' and Cboe Data Vantage3 Organic Net Revenue Growth Target2 to 'mid teens' from 'low teens'
•Decreases 2026 Adjusted Operating Expense Guidance2 to $830 to $845 million from $838 to $853 million
CHICAGO, IL – October 30, 2026 – Cboe Global Markets, Inc. (Cboe: CBOE) today reported financial results for the third quarter of 2026.
“Our strong momentum continued in the third quarter as we took important steps to advance our growth strategy,” said Craig Donohue, Chief Executive Officer of Cboe Global Markets. “First, we extended our exclusive S&P license through 2051, providing certainty and continuity for our SPX and VIX franchises. Second, building upon our history of innovation, we advanced our Cboe Predicts offering and our clearing capabilities through the quarter, and recently received the SEC approvals needed to list and clear our company-KPI event contracts, in anticipation of a November launch. Together, these accomplishments strengthen our core business and enhance our ability to drive growth and create shareholder value.”
“Cboe posted strong third quarter results, delivering net revenue growth of 18 percent year over year, diluted EPS rose 43 percent to a record $4.08, and adjusted diluted EPS¹ grew 36 percent to $3.63,” said Jill Griebenow, Cboe Global Markets Executive Vice President, Chief Financial Officer. “Net revenue in our Derivatives business rose 22 percent, driven by a 24 percent increase in index options average daily volume supported by record 0DTE activity. Cash and Spot Markets net revenue increased 12 percent, while Data Vantage delivered record quarterly net revenue, up 13 percent from a year ago. Given our strong year-to-date performance, we are increasing our 2026 total organic net revenue growth target2 to ‘high teens’ from ‘mid to high teens’, increasing our Data Vantage organic net revenue growth target2 to ‘mid teens’ from ‘low teens’, and lowering adjusted operating expense guidance to $830 to $845 million from $838 to $853 million.”

Discussion of Results by Business Segment:
Options:
•Options net revenue of $469.5 million was up $88.7 million, or 23 percent, from the third quarter of 2025. Net transaction and clearing fees1 increased primarily as a result of a 14 percent increase in total options average daily volume (“ADV”), coupled with a 9 percent increase in total options revenue per contract (“RPC”) versus the third quarter of 2025. Access and capacity fees were 25 percent higher and market data fees were 14 percent higher as compared to the third quarter of 2025.
•Net transaction and clearing fees1 increased $82.5 million, or 24 percent, reflecting a 24 percent increase in index options ADV and a 10 percent increase in multi-listed options ADV. Total options RPC increased 9 percent compared to the third quarter of 2025. The increase in total options RPC was due to a product mix shift, with index options representing a higher percentage of total options volume, paired with a 3 percent increase in index options RPC.
•Cboe’s Options exchanges had total market share of 30.5 percent for the third quarter of 2026, down compared to 30.9 percent in the third quarter of 2025.
North American (N.A.) Equities:
•N.A. Equities net revenue of $113.5 million increased $10.0 million, or 10 percent, from the third quarter of 2025, reflecting higher net transaction and clearing fees1 and access and capacity fees.
•Net transaction and clearing fees1 increased $4.9 million, or 16 percent, compared to the third quarter of 2025. The increase was driven by improved net capture rates for on-exchange U.S. Equities exchanges versus the third quarter of 2025.
•Cboe’s U.S. Equities exchanges had market share of 8.9 percent for the third quarter of 2026, down compared to 9.8 percent in the third quarter of 2025. Cboe’s U.S. Equities off-exchange market share was 21.3 percent, up from 17.9 percent in the third quarter of 2025.
Europe and Asia Pacific (APAC):
•Europe and APAC net revenue of $73.3 million increased $4.2 million, or 6 percent, from the third quarter of 2025, reflecting growth in net transaction and clearing fees1. On a constant currency basis2, net revenue was $74.1 million, up 7 percent on a year-over-year basis. European Equities average daily notional value (“ADNV”) traded on Cboe European Equities was €12.4 billion, up 6 percent compared to the third quarter of 2025 driven by stronger industry market volumes. Cboe Clear Europe net settlement volume reached 4,231.4 thousand shares, up 19 percent from the third quarter of 2025.
•For the third quarter of 2026, Cboe European Equities had 23.0 percent market share, down from 25.4 percent in the third quarter of 2025.
Futures:
•Futures net revenue of $28.2 million decreased $1.4 million, or 5 percent, from the third quarter of 2025 driven by a decrease in net transaction and clearing fees1.
•Net transaction and clearing fees1 decreased by $1.9 million, reflecting a 7 percent decrease in ADV when compared to the third quarter of 2025.
Global FX:
•Global FX net revenue of $27.7 million increased $5.2 million, or 23 percent, from the third quarter of 2025. The increase was due to higher net transaction and clearing fees1.
•ADNV traded on the Cboe FX platform was $61.1 billion for the quarter, up 22 percent compared to last year’s third quarter, and net capture rate per one million dollars traded was $2.91 for the third quarter of 2026, up 1 percent compared to $2.89 in the third quarter of 2025.

About Cboe Global Markets
Cboe Global Markets, Inc. is a leading global markets operator with a long history of innovation in equity derivatives. Since launching the world's first listed options exchange in 1973, Cboe has pioneered landmark products, including the introduction of S&P 500® index options and the creation of the VIX® Index, the world's leading gauge of market volatility, reshaping how investors manage risk and access opportunity. Today, Cboe operates derivatives, equities, and FX markets, providing trading, clearing, and investment solutions for customers worldwide. To learn more about Cboe, visit www.cboe.com.

ADV = average daily volume; ADNV = average daily notional value.
RPC, average revenue per contract, for options and futures, represents total net transaction fees recognized for the period divided by total contracts traded during the period.
Touched volume represents the total number of shares of equity securities and ETFs internally matched on our exchanges or routed to and executed on an external market center.
Matched volume represents the total number of shares of equity securities and ETFs executed on our exchanges.
U.S. Equities - Exchange, “net capture per 100 touched shares” refers to transaction fees less liquidity payments and routing and clearing costs divided by the product of one-hundredth ADV of touched shares on BZX, BYX, EDGX and EDGA and the number of trading days. U.S. Equities - Off-Exchange data reflects BIDS Trading. For U.S. Equities - Off-Exchange, “net capture per 100 touched shares” refers to transaction fees less order and execution management system (OMS/EMS) fees and clearing costs divided by the product of one-hundredth ADV of touched shares on BIDS Trading and the number of trading days for the period.
Canadian Equities, “net capture per 10,000 shares” refers to transaction fees divided by the product of one-ten-thousandth ADV of shares for Cboe Canada and the number of trading days. Total market share represents Cboe Canada volume divided by the total volume of the Canadian Equities market.
European Equities, “net capture per matched notional value” refers to transaction fees less liquidity payments in Euros divided by the product of ADNV in Euros of shares matched on Cboe Europe Equities and the number of trading days. “Trades cleared” refers to the total number of non-interoperable trades cleared, “Fee per trade cleared” refers to clearing fees divided by the number of non-interoperable trades cleared, “Net settlement volume” refers to the total number of settlements executed after netting, and “Net fee per settlement” refers to settlement fees less direct costs incurred to settle divided by the number of settlements executed after netting.
Global FX, “net capture per one million dollars traded” refers to transaction fees less liquidity payments, if any, divided by the Spot and SEF products of one-thousandth of ADNV traded on the Cboe FX Markets and the number of trading days, divided by two, which represents the buyer and seller that are both charged on the transaction.
Average transaction fees per contract can be affected by various factors, including exchange fee rates, volume-based discounts, and transaction mix by contract type and product type.

EBITDA Reconciliations
EBITDA (earnings before interest, income taxes, depreciation and amortization) and Adjusted EBITDA are widely used non-GAAP financial measures of operating performance. These metrics are presented as supplemental information that the company believes are useful to investors to evaluate the company's results because they exclude certain items that are not directly related to the company’s core operating performance. Operating EBITDA is calculated by adding back to operating income depreciation and amortization. Adjusted Operating EBITDA is calculated by adding back to Operating EBITDA relevant adjustments. Operating EBITDA margin represents Operating EBITDA divided by revenues less cost of revenues. Adjusted Operating EBITDA margin represents Adjusted Operating EBITDA divided by revenues less cost of revenues. EBITDA is calculated by adding back to net income interest (income) expense, net, income tax expense, and depreciation and amortization. EBITDA margin represents EBITDA divided by revenues less cost of revenues. Adjusted EBITDA is calculated by adding back to EBITDA relevant adjustments. Adjusted EBITDA margin represents Adjusted EBITDA divided by revenues less cost of revenues. Relevant adjustments are detailed in the reconciliations that follow. Operating EBITDA, Adjusted Operating EBITDA, EBITDA, and Adjusted EBITDA should not be considered as substitutes either for net income, as an indicator of the company’s operating performance, or for cash flow as a measure of the company’s liquidity. In addition, because Operating EBITDA, Operating EBITDA margin, Adjusted Operating EBITDA, Adjusted Operating EBITDA margin, EBITDA, EBITDA margin, Adjusted EBITDA, and Adjusted EBITDA margin may not be calculated identically by all companies, the presentation here may not be comparable to other similarly titled measures of other companies.
