PSQ Holdings Announces Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results and Reaffirms $32 Million Full-Year Revenue Outlook

 

·Third Quarter Revenue Grows 67% to $7.3 million; Year-to-Date Revenue More Than Doubles to $22.6 million

·GAAP Operating Loss Improves 66% to $3.3 million; Non-GAAP Operating Income of $0.3 million

·Third Quarter Revenue Per Headcount Improves 243%; Year-to-Date improves 328%

·Completes Sale of EveryLife, Concluding Exit from Consumer Brands

·Material Weakness Remediated

 

BOZEMAN, MT, November 6, 2026 — PSQ Holdings, Inc. (NYSE: PSQH) (the “Company”), a payments and financial infrastructure company, today reported financial results for the third quarter 2026. With the sale of EveryLife completed, the Company has concluded its exit from consumer brands and now operates exclusively in payments and financial infrastructure.

 

“In July, I said the results are the argument and asked you to hold us to our numbers. The third quarter adds to that record,” said Dusty Wunderlich, Chairman and Chief Executive Officer of PSQ Holdings. “Revenue grew 67%, operating expenses fell by half, and we delivered our second consecutive quarter of positive non-GAAP operating income. Our operating loss and the cash we use in operations have improved every quarter this year, and cash used in operations fell to $1.7 million from $6.1 million a year ago. With EveryLife sold, our exit from consumer brands is complete, and every part of this company now moves money. We are reaffirming $32 million in revenue and positive non-GAAP operating income for 2026, and we continue to work toward positive monthly operating cash flow in 2027. We add nothing back until it earns its way in.”

 

 

 

 

THIRD QUARTER 2026 HIGHLIGHTS

 

·Net revenue from continuing operations, which includes the financial technology (“fintech”) segment, for the three months ended September 30, 2026 was $7.3 million compared to $4.4 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025, a 67% increase compared to the prior year period.

·Operating expense (defined as general and administrative, sales and marketing, and research and development expense) for the three months ended September 30, 2026 decreased $5.4 million or a decrease of 50% compared to the prior year period.

·Operating loss for the three months ended September 30, 2026 was $3.3 million, an improvement of $6.4 million or 66% compared to $9.7 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025.

·Net cash used in operating activities for the three months ended September 30, 2026 was $1.7 million, an improvement of $4.4 million or 72% compared to $6.1 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025.

·Loss from discontinued operations, net of tax for the three months ended September 30, 2026 was $0.1 million compared to $1.9 million for the same period in 2025.

·Net loss for the three months ended September 30, 2026 was $4.1 million, an improvement of $7.9 million, or 66%, compared to a net loss of $12.0 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025.

·Loss per share for the three months ended September 30, 2026 improved to $1.00 compared to $3.90 for the three months ended September 30, 2025, a 74% improvement.

·Revenue per headcount for the three months ended September 30, 2026 was $209,800 compared to $61,179 for the three months ended September 30, 2025, an improvement of 243%. Revenue per headcount is calculated as total revenue divided by full-time equivalent employees as of the last day of the period.

·Non-GAAP operating income (a non-GAAP measure) for the three months ended September 30, 2026 was $0.3 million compared to non-GAAP operating loss of $2.2 million in the prior year period, an improvement of $2.5 million or 115%.

 

YEAR TO DATE 2026 HIGHLIGHTS

 

·Net revenue from continuing operations, which includes the financial technology (“fintech”) segment, for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 was $22.6 million compared to $10.9 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2025, a 108% increase compared to the prior year period.

·Operating expense (defined as general and administrative, sales and marketing, and research and development expense) for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 decreased $6.4 million or a decrease of 23% compared to the prior year period.

·Operating loss for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 was $14.3 million, an improvement of $10.0 million or 41% compared to $24.2 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2025.

·Net cash used in operating activities for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 was $8.2 million, an improvement of $9.2 million or 53% compared to $17.4 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2025.

·Loss from discontinued operations, net of tax for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 was $0.5 million compared to $7.2 million for the same period in 2025.

·Net loss for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 was $16.2 million, an improvement of $8.6 million, or 35%, compared to a net loss of $24.8 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2025.

·Loss per share for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 decreased to $4.29 compared to $8.31 for the same period in 2025, a 48% improvement.

·Revenue from continued operations per headcount for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 was $646,684 compared to $151,216 for the nine months ended September 30, 2025, an improvement of 328%.

·Non-GAAP operating loss (a non-GAAP measure) for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 was $0.1 million compared to a non-GAAP operating loss of $7.8 million loss in the prior year period, an improvement of $7.7 million or 98%.

 

The definitions and reconciliations of non-GAAP operating income (loss) to GAAP operating loss are provided under the heading “Non-GAAP Financial Measures” at the end of this release.

MATERIAL WEAKNESS REMEDIATED

 

As previously disclosed, the Company reported a material weakness in its internal control over financial reporting, first identified as of September 30, 2023, related to presentation on the statements of cash flows. To remediate the material weakness, management expanded and enhanced its review process for complex transactions and added accounting professionals with the requisite experience. Following testing of the design and operating effectiveness of these controls, management concluded that the material weakness had been remediated as of September 30, 2026.

 

2026 OUTLOOK

 

The Company reaffirms its outlook for full-year 2026 net revenue from continuing operations of

 

$32 million and positive non-GAAP operating income for the full year. Through the first nine months of 2026, the Company generated net revenue from continuing operations of $22.6 million and a non-GAAP operating loss of $0.1 million, including positive non-GAAP operating income in both the second and third quarters.

 

The Company expects fourth-quarter 2026 revenue to benefit from seasonal demand, as hunting season and the holiday period typically increase consumer financing and payment volume across its core firearms and outdoor merchant verticals. The Company also anticipates fourth-quarter contributions from loan payment processing through its relationships with LendSuite, Dealer Cash and Roja, each announced during the third quarter, and expects these relationships to continue to build into 2027.

 

COMPLETION OF BRANDS SEGMENT DIVESTITURE

 

On November 5, 2026, the Company completed the sale of EveryLife, its direct-to-consumer diaper and baby products brand, to FreeHold Brands, LLC (“Buyer”), for gross proceeds of $5.5 million, including $1.0 million in cash and a $4.5 million seller note by Buyer, before transaction fees and customary adjustments. Please refer to our Current Report on Form 8-K filed on November 5, 2026 for more information.

 

The Company has reported EveryLife as discontinued operations since the third quarter of 2025, reflecting the Company's previously stated intention to divest non-core assets as it concentrates on its core payments and financial infrastructure businesses.

 

FINANCIAL REVIEW

 

Balance Sheet & Liquidity

 

·As of September 30, 2026, the Company had $6.8 million of restricted cash and cash and cash equivalents, which included $1.2 million related to discontinued operations.

·The Company had an outstanding principal balance of $6.8 million on its $10.0 million revolving line of credit as of September 30, 2026. The Company draws on this credit line to fund new consumer loan and lease originations, and repays it as those loans are collected or sold to third parties.
