Dragonfly Energy Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results

 

Heavy-Duty Trucking Revenue More Than Doubled Sequentially as Fleet Adoption Accelerates

 

Trucking Momentum Expected to Continue, With Q4 2026 Trucking Revenue Projected to Nearly Double on a Sequential Basis

 

Completed Acquisition of Dakota Lithium Brand, Expanding Into New Markets and Broadening Product Portfolio

 

Provides Q4 2026 Guidance of Approximately Breakeven Adjusted EBITDA

 

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Summary

 

  ● Net sales were $12.2 million
  ● OEM net sales were $7.1 million
  ● Gross Margin was 27.0%
  ● Net Loss Attributable to Common Stockholders was $(6.7) million
  ● Adjusted EBITDA was $(2.3) million

 

RENO, NEVADA (November 12, 2026) — Dragonfly Energy Holdings Corp. (Nasdaq: DFLI) (“Dragonfly Energy” or the “Company”), an industry leader in lithium battery technology, today reported its financial and operational results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.

 

“Third quarter heavy-duty trucking revenue more than doubled sequentially to $1.3 million, in line with our August outlook, driven by expanding commercial fleet adoption,” commented Dr. Denis Phares, Chief Executive Officer. “For the first time, we received orders from fleets moving directly into commercial deployment without a pilot program, reflecting the credibility we have established through years of field validation. Based on our current order book, we expect trucking revenue to nearly double again in the fourth quarter on a sequential basis. Persistently high diesel prices are also driving greater urgency among fleets seeking to offset fuel costs, and we believe Dragonfly’s commercial trucking portfolio positions us well to capitalize on this opportunity.”

 

“RV market conditions remained challenging, which drove net sales below our expectations. However, adoption within our premium OEM partnerships remains healthy, as customers continue to standardize lithium systems and increase energy storage content across their product lineups.”

 

“Following our acquisition of Dakota Lithium at the end of July, we have been rebuilding inventory, re-engaging its dealer and distribution network, and strengthening its e-commerce capabilities. Early demand has been encouraging. We expect new inventory to begin arriving toward the end of the fourth quarter, with broader availability and a more meaningful revenue contribution beginning in the first quarter of 2027. We also expect Dakota to be accretive to Adjusted EBITDA as we leverage our existing infrastructure with minimal incremental overhead.”

Net sales were $12.2 million, below the Company’s previously issued guidance of approximately $13.5 million, and included $7.1 million in OEM net sales and $4.8 million in DTC net sales. The decline in OEM net sales reflects ongoing softness in the RV industry, where wholesale shipments were down 13.7% year-to-date through September, according to the RV Industry Association. Despite that backdrop, adoption of our solutions continued to broaden. DTC net sales declined 4.7%, reflecting continued macroeconomic pressures.

 

Gross profit was $3.3 million, with a gross margin of 27.0%, compared to gross profit of $4.7 million and a gross margin of 29.7% in the third quarter of 2025. The year-over-year declines were primarily driven by lower volumes. Operating expenses totaled $7.3 million, compared to $8.5 million a year ago, reflecting progress on the Company’s cost-reduction initiatives. The Company continues to pursue additional operational efficiencies.

 

The Company reported a Net Loss of $(5.5) million and a Net Loss Attributable to Common Stockholders of $(6.7) million, or $(3.71) per diluted share. This compares to a Net Loss and Net Loss Attributable to Common Stockholders of $(11.1) million, or $(16.45) per diluted share, in the third quarter of 2025.

 

Adjusted EBITDA, excluding stock-based compensation, changes in the fair market value of our warrants, and other one-time expenses, was $(2.3) million, compared to $(2.1) million in the third quarter of 2025. The $0.2 million year-over-year decline in Adjusted EBITDA, despite a 23.8% decrease in net sales, reflects the progress we have made through our cost-reduction initiatives. Sequentially, Adjusted EBITDA declined $0.7 million from $(1.6) million in the second quarter of 2026.

 

Summary and Outlook

 

“Looking ahead to the fourth quarter, we expect trucking revenue to nearly double sequentially as existing fleet customers expand their deployments and we onboard new fleets. While RV market conditions remain challenging, we expect continued progress in trucking and Dakota Lithium, combined with our ongoing cost-reduction initiatives, to support our path toward profitability. We also anticipate broader product availability and a more meaningful revenue contribution from Dakota Lithium beginning in the first quarter of 2027, with the acquisition expected to be accretive to Adjusted EBITDA as we leverage our existing infrastructure. We remain focused on achieving sustainable positive Adjusted EBITDA, supported by continued growth in trucking, Dakota Lithium, and ongoing cost efficiencies,” concluded Dr. Phares.

Use of Non-GAAP Financial Measures

 

Adjusted EBITDA is a non-GAAP measure and should be considered only as supplemental to, and not as superior to, financial measures prepared in accordance with United States generally accepted accounting principles (“GAAP”). Please refer to the reconciliation of Adjusted EBITDA to its nearest GAAP measure in this release.

 

The Company provides non-GAAP financial measures including EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA as a supplement to GAAP financial information to enhance the overall understanding of the Company’s financial performance and to assist investors in evaluating the Company’s results of operations, period over period. Adjusted non-GAAP measures exclude significant unusual items. Investors should consider these non-GAAP measures as a supplement to, and not a substitute for, financial information prepared on a GAAP basis.

 

EBITDA is defined as earnings before interest and other income (expenses), income taxes, and depreciation and amortization. Adjusted EBITDA is calculated as EBITDA adjusted for stock-based compensation, change in fair market value of warrant liabilities, non-recurring costs associated with strategic financing, reverse stock split, litigation and loss on settlement. Adjusted EBITDA is a performance measure that the Company believes is useful to investors and analysts because it illustrates the underlying financial and business trends relating to the Company’s core, recurring results of operations and enhances comparability between periods.

 

Adjusted EBITDA has limitations as an analytical tool, and it should not be considered in isolation or as a substitute for analysis of net loss or other results as reported under GAAP. Some of these limitations are:

 

● Adjusted EBITDA does not reflect the Company’s cash expenditures, future requirements for capital expenditures, or contractual commitments;
   
● Adjusted EBITDA does not reflect changes in, or cash requirements for, the Company’s working capital needs;
   
● Adjusted EBITDA does not reflect the Company’s tax expense or the cash requirements to pay taxes;
   
● Although amortization and depreciation are non-cash charges, the assets being amortized and depreciated will often have to be replaced in the future and Adjusted EBITDA does not reflect any cash requirements for such replacements;
   
● Adjusted EBITDA should not be construed as an inference that the Company’s future results will be unaffected by unusual or non-recurring items for which the Company may adjust in historical periods; and
   
● Other companies in the industry may calculate Adjusted EBITDA differently than the Company does, limiting its usefulness as a comparative measure.

About Dragonfly Energy

 

Dragonfly Energy Holdings Corp. (Nasdaq: DFLI) is a lithium battery technology company spanning battery cell manufacturing, pack assembly and full-system integration. The Company develops and delivers energy storage solutions for mobile, off-grid, industrial and specialty applications.

 

Dragonfly Energy is advancing domestic battery cell manufacturing through its patented dry electrode process and the development of next-generation battery technologies, including all-solid-state battery cells. Its work combines advanced research and development with software-enabled intelligence to improve the performance and capabilities of energy storage systems.

 

To learn more about Dragonfly Energy and its commitment to clean energy advancements, visit https://investors.dragonflyenergy.com/.
