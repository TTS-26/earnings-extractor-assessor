WINNEBAGO INDUSTRIES REPORTS FOURTH QUARTER AND FULL YEAR FISCAL 2026 RESULTS
-- Motorhome RV Continues to Gain Share through Innovation and Operational Execution(1) --
-- Recent Manufacturing Footprint Actions Expected to Drive Long-Term Returns --
-- Barletta Continues to Expand Share of the U.S. Aluminum Pontoon Market(1) --
-- Strong Cash Flows in the Quarter and an Improvement in Net Leverage to 2.2x --
-- Company Issues Fiscal 2027 Financial Guidance --
EDEN PRAIRIE, MINN., October 21, 2026 -- Winnebago Industries, Inc. (NYSE: WGO), a leading manufacturer of outdoor recreation products, today reported financial results for the Company's fourth quarter and full year Fiscal 2026.
Fourth Quarter Fiscal 2026 Financial Summary
•Net revenues of $645.1 million compared to $777.3 million in the fourth quarter of Fiscal 2025
•Gross profit of $80.7 million, representing 12.5% gross margin, compared to $99.2 million, representing 12.8% gross margin in the fourth quarter of Fiscal 2025
•Net income of $9.7 million, or $0.34 per diluted share; adjusted earnings per diluted share of $0.49 compared to $0.71 in the fourth quarter of Fiscal 2025
•Adjusted EBITDA of $32.6 million, representing 5.1% adjusted EBITDA margin
•Net cash flow from operations of $142.5 million
•Net leverage ratio improved to 2.2x at quarter end
Full Year Fiscal 2026 Financial Summary
•Net revenues of $2,703.9 million compared to $2,798.2 million in Fiscal 2025
•Gross profit of $350.2 million, representing 13.0% gross margin, compared to $365.1 million, representing 13.0% gross margin in Fiscal 2025
•Net income of $34.5 million, or $1.21 per diluted share; adjusted earnings per diluted share of $1.79 compared to $1.67 in Fiscal 2025
•Adjusted EBITDA of $125.0 million, representing 4.6% adjusted EBITDA margin
•Net cash flow from operations of $168.7 million
CEO Commentary
“Fiscal 2026 highlighted the resilience of our portfolio and the disciplined execution of our teams in a persistently challenging retail environment,” said President and Chief Executive Officer Michael Happe. “By maintaining focus on the factors within our control, including product innovation, operational improvements and deliberate production and inventory management, we continued to strengthen our brands and position the portfolio for long-term growth.
1
“Even as market pressure across our segments persisted, we began to see encouraging early signs across several strategic initiatives: real traction in the Winnebago Motorhome transformation, stabilizing share trends and improving market share at Grand Design Towables, growing market penetration for Grand Design Motorized, and impressive share growth at Winnebago Towables,” Happe said. “We took actions to rationalize capacity, drive operational efficiency, and better align our manufacturing footprint with market demand. These initiatives remain at different stages of development, and we expect their financial contribution to build gradually, with greater value accruing in future years. Together with the strong continued growth of Barletta's market presence in pontoons and our growing portable power offerings, this progress reinforces our confidence in the long-term earnings potential of our portfolio.
"At multiple retail, trade, and dealer meeting events in September, we demonstrated the breadth of our portfolio with new offerings across brands, product categories and price points. Dealer feedback was encouraging and reinforced that the investments we've made around both innovation and affordability are well aligned with evolving consumer preferences, positioning us to capitalize on opportunities within our control while continuing to elevate the ownership experience."
Fourth Quarter Fiscal 2026 Results
Net revenues were $645.1 million, a decrease of 17.0% compared to $777.3 million in the fourth quarter of Fiscal 2025, primarily due to lower unit volume and product mix, partially offset by selective price adjustments.
Gross profit was $80.7 million, a decrease of 18.6% compared to $99.2 million in the fourth quarter of Fiscal 2025. Gross profit margin decreased 30 basis points in the quarter to 12.5%, primarily due to volume deleverage, partially offset by operational efficiencies from Motorhome segment transformation initiatives.
Operating expenses were $63.1 million, a decrease of 20.1% compared to $79.1 million in the fourth quarter of Fiscal 2025, primarily due to cost reduction initiatives.
Operating income was $17.6 million, a decrease of 12.3% compared to $20.1 million in the fourth quarter of Fiscal 2025.
Net income was $9.7 million, compared to $13.7 million in the fourth quarter of Fiscal 2025. Reported earnings per diluted share was $0.34, compared to a reported earnings per diluted share of $0.49 in the fourth quarter of Fiscal 2025. Adjusted earnings per diluted share was $0.49, a decrease of 31.0% compared to adjusted earnings per diluted share of $0.71 in the fourth quarter of Fiscal 2025.
Consolidated Adjusted EBITDA was $32.6 million, a decrease of 14.7%, compared to $38.2 million in the fourth quarter of Fiscal 2025.
Full Year Fiscal 2026 Results
Net revenues were $2.7 billion, a decrease of 3.4% compared to $2.8 billion in Fiscal 2025, primarily due to lower unit volume, partially offset by selective price adjustments and product mix.
Gross profit was $350.2 million, a decrease of 4.1% compared to $365.1 million in Fiscal 2025. Gross profit margin remained consistent year-over-year at 13.0% as volume deleverage was largely offset by selective price adjustments.
Operating expenses were $284.0 million, compared to $307.9 million in Fiscal 2025. Operating expenses decreased primarily due to cost reduction initiatives, partially offset by costs to support the growth of the Grand Design Motorhome business.
Operating income was $66.2 million, an increase of 15.7% compared to $57.2 million in Fiscal 2025.
2
Net income was $34.5 million, compared to $25.7 million in Fiscal 2025. Reported earnings per diluted share was $1.21, an increase of 33.0% compared to reported earnings per diluted share of $0.91 in Fiscal 2025. Adjusted earnings per diluted share was $1.79, an increase of 7.2% compared to adjusted earnings per diluted share of $1.67 in Fiscal 2025.
Consolidated Adjusted EBITDA was $125.0 million, an increase of 2.5%, compared to $121.9 million in Fiscal 2025.

Balance Sheet and Cash Flow
At the end of the fourth quarter of Fiscal 2026, cash and cash equivalents totaled $181.4 million compared to $57.1 million at the end of the Fiscal 2026 third quarter and $174.0 million at the end of Fiscal 2025. Total outstanding debt was $443.3 million, which included $450.0 million of debt, net of issuance costs of $6.7 million. Working capital improved to $419.1 million, reflecting disciplined balance sheet management and stronger operating efficiency. Cash flow provided by operations was $142.5 million in the Fiscal 2026 fourth quarter. Net leverage was 2.2x at the end of the Fiscal 2026 fourth quarter, down from 3.0x at the end of the third quarter, reflecting strong cash generation and continued progress in strengthening our leverage position.
Quarterly Cash Dividend and Share Repurchases
On August 12, 2026, the Company’s Board of Directors approved a 3% increase in the quarterly cash dividend to $0.36 per share. The dividend was paid on September 23, 2026, to common stockholders of record at the close of business on September 9, 2026.
“Fiscal 2026 also reflected the strength of our balance sheet and disciplined approach to capital allocation,” Happe said. “The renewal and extension of our $350 million revolving credit facility through 2031 and the Board's decision to increase the quarterly dividend underscore our confidence in the business and long-term strategy. Supported by solid operating cash flow, improved working-capital performance and lower year-end leverage, we exited the year with a stronger balance sheet and the flexibility to continue investing in innovation, operations and customer experience."
Outlook
For calendar year 2026, Winnebago Industries now expects North American RV wholesale shipments in the range of 275,000 to 295,000 units. For calendar year 2027, Winnebago Industries anticipates total North American RV wholesale shipments in the range of 265,000 to 295,000 units. Based on this outlook and the current business environment, in Fiscal 2027 the Company expects:
•Consolidated net revenues in the range of $2.65 to $2.80 billion;
•Reported earnings per diluted share in the range of $0.90 to $1.50; and
•Adjusted earnings per diluted share in the range of $1.65 to $2.25(2)
The Company’s outlook takes into account prevailing trends in the RV sector, including the impacts from current policy and trade environment, competitive dynamics, shifts in consumer preferences, and key macroeconomic factors that may influence overall
demand.

About Winnebago Industries
Winnebago Industries, Inc. is a leading North American manufacturer of outdoor recreation products under the Winnebago, Grand Design, Chris-Craft, Newmar and Barletta brands, which are used primarily in leisure travel and outdoor recreation activities. The Company builds high-quality motorhomes, travel trailers, fifth-wheel products, outboard and sterndrive powerboats, pontoons, and commercial community outreach vehicles. Committed to advancing sustainable innovation and leveraging vertical integration in key component areas, Winnebago Industries has multiple facilities in Iowa, Indiana, Minnesota and Florida. The Company’s common stock is listed on the New York Stock Exchange and traded under the symbol WGO.
